## Full description

### English

Amar Dokan POS is a complete shop manager for Bangladeshi small
businesses — grocery, pharmacy, clothing, electronics, and restaurants.

- **Sell fast:** POS billing with cash change, discounts, VAT, and
  printed or Bluetooth receipts.
- **Know your stock:** live stock levels, low-stock and expiry alerts,
  dead-stock view, and purchase orders to suppliers.
- **Track dues:** customer dues, supplier dues, and daily cash
  reconciliation per counter (Z-report).
- **Work offline:** sell without internet; sales sync automatically
  when you reconnect — nothing is lost.
- **Sell online:** customers can order from your store's menu or
  marketplace listing; you confirm, prepare, and deliver.
- **Bangla first:** full Bangla and English interface, Bangla voice
  commands at the counter, and Bangla receipts.
- **Staff control:** give each staff member only the permissions they
  need — sales, inventory, reports, or expenses.
- **Your data stays yours:** PIN-protected login, per-store data
  separation, and nightly database backups.

No card or mobile-banking payment is captured inside the app unless
your shop enables the gateway callback flow with its own merchant
credentials; by default, mobile payments are confirmed by you
out-of-band and recorded as a reference only.
